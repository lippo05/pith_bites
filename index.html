The Cookie Experience

About us: so, who are we?

PithBites – sustainable, nutritious, delicious

We are a sustainable food innovation venture that aims to transform underutilized durian pith into a value-added, fibre-enriched cookie.

Presented by Haseen & Huda. Pith Bites is presented by Greenfork Lab.

Key features

  • Fibre-enriched snack
  • Made from upcycled durian pith
  • Natural and locally inspired ingredients
  • Unique texture and taste
  • Convenient ready-to-eat format
  • Made with high quality ingredients

What sets us apart?

Our USP: Overlooked durian pith to an everyday snack. The cookie, reimagined with an unconventional, Malaysian twist.

Pith Bites transforms upcycled durian pith into Pith Powder, then incorporates it into a delicious cookie format that is familiar and easy to enjoy. This creates value from an overlooked material while supporting more sustainable food innovation.

Our solution? Meet PithBites

Upcycling durian pith

  • Transform the by-product into a usable food ingredient
  • Gives it a second use instead of treating it solely as waste

Turning by-products into food value

  • Process it into a value-added ingredient
  • Create a new application + potential economic value

Reimagining the everyday cookie

  • Innovative cookie that combines familiar snacking with an unconventional, upcycled ingredient
  • Distinctive snack option that connects taste, nutrition, and sustainability

The Durian Problem

Three identified problems

Agricultural by-product waste

  • Processing generates substantial amounts of biomass
  • Much of this material has limited application

Underutilized food resource

  • Valuable biomass is usually overlooked

Conventional snack choices

  • Consumers have increasing interest in foods that combine convenience, nutrition, and sustainability
  • Available products might not be appealing or enjoyable
  • Large amounts of durian pith are often discarded as waste
  • Consumers are seeking healthier and more sustainable snack options
  • There is limited availability of local fibre-enriched snacks
